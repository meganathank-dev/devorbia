DevOrbia solves a **real software-development organization management problem**: information, people, projects, tasks, workload, deadlines, and organizational decisions are often scattered across different tools.

### 🎯 The main problem

A software company may have:

```text
Employees       → one place
Teams           → another place
Projects        → another tool
Tasks           → another tool
Leave            → another process
Workload        → spreadsheets
Performance     → manual tracking
Reports         → manual preparation
```

Because these systems are disconnected, managers often don't have a complete picture.

---

# 🚨 Problems DevOrbia Solves

### 1. Fragmented organization management

It connects:

**Organization → Department → Team → Employee → Project → Task**

So management can understand **who belongs where and what they are working on**.

---

### 2. Poor task assignment

A manager may assign a task simply because:

> "This employee is available."

DevOrbia considers:

* Skills
* Experience
* Workload
* Availability
* Project familiarity
* Deadline
* Task complexity

and provides an **AI-powered recommendation**.

---

### 3. Employee overload

Example:

```text
Employee A → 95% workload
Employee B → 40%
Employee C → 55%
```

Without proper visibility, A may continue receiving tasks.

**AI Workload Balancing** identifies this imbalance and suggests redistribution.

---

### 4. Projects becoming delayed without early warning

A project may appear to be progressing normally until the deadline is very close.

DevOrbia analyzes:

* Overdue tasks
* Blocked tasks
* Dependencies
* Progress
* Workload
* Deadlines
* Leave
* Team capacity

and identifies:

> 🚨 **Project is at risk**

before the problem becomes critical.

---

### 5. Employee leave affecting projects

Suppose:

```text
Employee → Leave for 5 days
             ↓
3 active tasks
             ↓
1 critical deadline
             ↓
Project may be delayed
```

Instead of discovering this manually, DevOrbia performs **Leave Impact Analysis**.

It can recommend suitable replacements.

---

### 6. Difficulty understanding team performance

Managers often judge performance using simple metrics like:

> "Who completed the most tasks?"

That's not necessarily fair.

DevOrbia can consider:

* Completion
* On-time delivery
* Task complexity
* Consistency
* Contribution
* Improvement
* Project involvement

and provide **Performance & Recognition Intelligence**.

---

### 7. Lack of motivation

Employees may not know how their contribution compares with the team's overall performance.

DevOrbia can provide meaningful recognition such as:

🏆 Best Employee
🏆 Best Team
🏆 Best Team Leader
📈 Most Improved
⭐ Outstanding Contribution

using defined metrics rather than arbitrary selection.

---

### 8. Poor project visibility

A Project Manager shouldn't have to manually check hundreds of tasks.

DevOrbia provides:

```text
Project Health
     ↓
Progress
     ↓
Tasks
     ↓
Overdue work
     ↓
Blocked work
     ↓
Team workload
     ↓
Deadlines
```

in one place.

---

### 9. Security and organization isolation

This is a major problem with multi-tenant systems.

If:

```text
Company A
```

uses DevOrbia, its users must never see:

```text
Company B
```

data.

DevOrbia therefore solves:

**cross-organization data leakage and unauthorized resource access** through backend-enforced tenant isolation and RBAC.
